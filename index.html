What happens when your team can't answer

Three participants, one workflow. Here is what each one does.

📞 1. Caller

Reaches an automated receptionist

It answers immediately, discloses that it's automated, and asks relevant questions about the HVAC problem — issue, urgency, contact info, and service location.

🧠 2. Lead Rescue

Extracts, checks, and routes

It extracts the contact and service details, checks them against the business's own rules and service area, and decides the right next action — on-call, callback, or estimate.

🛠️ 3. Owner

Gets a ready-to-act lead

A structured lead summary, a callback or appointment request, and a full transcript with disposition — so you can call back and book the job instead of chasing details.

Trust and safety

  • Lead Rescue does not pretend to be a human. Callers are told up front that they're speaking with an automated receptionist.
  • For medical, fire, or gas-leak emergencies, the system does not give technical advice — it routes the caller toward emergency services or your configured emergency procedure.
  • We do not sell caller data to third parties.
  • You control the script: what Lead Rescue asks, how it responds, and when it hands off to your team.
  • Transcript and recording disclosure is configured to match applicable law and your business's policy — this page does not make broad legal guarantees.
